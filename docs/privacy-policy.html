Privacy Policy

Five By Five Lifting (com.fivebyfivelifting.app) • Effective Date: November 21, 2026
Summary: Five By Five Lifting stores all workout data locally on your device. We do not operate backend servers, display ads, sell user data, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores your workout logs, custom templates, working weights, rest timer preference, and optional bodyweight entries in a local SQLite database and Android DataStore preferences directly on your phone. This data stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up, export it, or sync it to Android Health Connect.

2. Optional Android Auto-Backup & Google Drive Export

If you enable Android device backup or manually save/export a JSON or CSV backup file through Android’s system file picker or the Google Drive app, that file is transmitted directly between your device and your own chosen storage destination. We never receive a copy of your backup files or have remote access to your Google Drive.

3. Optional Google Sign-In

If you choose to tap Sign in with Google in Settings, the app requests only basic account identity information (email address, display name, and account ID) solely to display which account is connected inside the app and pre-select your Google account when saving a backup file to Google Drive. Your profile info is stored only on your device inside app preferences and is never transmitted to our servers. You can sign out at any time from Settings to clear it from the app.

4. Optional Android Health Connect Integration

Five By Five Lifting integrates optionally with Android Health Connect using a single write-only permission: android.permission.health.WRITE_EXERCISE.

  • What is written: Only when you explicitly enable Health Connect sync in Settings, the app writes completed weightlifting sessions (workout title, start and end timestamps, and set/volume summary notes) as ExerciseSessionRecord entries.
  • What is NOT accessed: The app does not read exercise history, heart rate, sleep, nutrition, or any other records from Health Connect.
  • Limited Use Policy: Health Connect data is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, employment eligibility, credit, or insurance underwriting, and is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Revoking access: You may disable Health Connect sync in Settings or revoke permission anytime in Android’s Health Connect system settings.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

This app contains no advertisements and no third-party analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.

6. Deleting Your Data

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over deletion:

  • Delete individual completed workouts directly from the History tab.
  • Clear signed-in Google account info using Sign Out in Settings.
  • Delete all app data by clearing storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Delete any exported Drive JSON/CSV files directly from your Google Drive, and delete synced exercise records inside Android Health Connect settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is intended for general strength training audiences (ages 13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes & Contact

Any future updates to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. If you have questions or requests regarding privacy, please open an issue or reach out via the developer contact listed on the Google Play Store listing or repository: github.com/sunil2kalva/five-by-five-lifting-android.
